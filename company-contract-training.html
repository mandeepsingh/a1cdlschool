How It Works

A clear path from day one to a CDL, with the company covering the cost of training.

  1. 01

    Get a DOT Physical

    Drivers get a DOT physical and medical examiner's certificate before training starts.

  2. 02

    Prep for the Permit

    Permit prep gets drivers ready for the CDL knowledge tests.

  3. 03

    Pass the Permit Test

    Drivers pass the knowledge tests at the BMV to get their Commercial Learner's Permit.

  4. 04

    Start CDL Training

    Theory and behind-the-wheel training, scheduled around your drivers' work hours.

  5. 05

    Take the Skills Test

    Once our instructors sign off, drivers take the CDL skills test.

Company Contract FAQs

Answers to the questions employers ask us most.

What is company contract training?

It's CDL training paid for by a company for an employee or future employee. It's a good fit for trucking companies, construction and utility companies, owner-operators, and anyone starting their own trucking company.

How does employer-sponsored training work?

The company enrolls the driver and pays for training. We schedule the training, guide the driver from permit to skills test, and keep you updated along the way.

How does the cost compare to a traditional CDL school?

Full-time CDL programs often cost $5,000 to $10,000 and require weeks of full-time attendance. Our company packages focus on the training your drivers need, so you pay less and they spend less time away from work.

Is the training ELDT compliant?

Yes. Our theory and behind-the-wheel training meet the FMCSA Entry-Level Driver Training (ELDT) requirements.

Can training fit around my drivers' work schedules?

Yes. Training is often scheduled in 4 to 5 hour blocks over several weeks, so drivers can keep working while they train.

Where does training take place?

At our training yard at 2700 W National Rd in Dayton, or on-site at your location.

Can you customize a package for our company?

Yes. We can build a package around your drivers' experience, the equipment they'll drive, and your schedule. Contact us for a custom quote.
