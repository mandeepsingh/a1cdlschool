Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Overview

A1 CDL School ("A1 CDL School," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website or contact us, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By using our website, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you submit our Request Information form, call, email or message us, we may collect:

  • Your name, phone number and email address
  • The program you are interested in
  • Anything you include in your message

If you enroll, we collect additional information needed for training and testing, such as your date of birth, driver's license and permit details, DOT medical certification, and payment information. This is covered by your enrollment agreement and the records requirements that apply to CDL training providers.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider may record basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, and the pages you view. We do not use advertising cookies or sell this information.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your questions and send you program, scheduling and pricing information
  • To enroll you, schedule training, and manage your student record
  • To meet federal and state requirements, including reporting training completion to the FMCSA Training Provider Registry
  • To process payments and keep accurate business records
  • To keep our website secure and working properly

Calls, texts and email

If you give us your phone number or email address, we may contact you about your inquiry or enrollment. You can ask us to stop at any time by replying STOP to a text message, using the unsubscribe link in an email, or contacting us directly. Message and data rates may apply.

How we share information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only when needed to provide our services or when required by law, for example with:

  • Government agencies such as the FMCSA and the Ohio Bureau of Motor Vehicles, as required for CDL training and testing
  • Service providers who help us run our business, such as payment processors, email and website hosting providers, who may use your information only to perform services for us
  • Employers or recruiters, only with your permission
  • Law enforcement or others when required by law or to protect our rights and safety

Third-party services

Our website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers and Google may receive your IP address. Our contact page shows an embedded Google Map, which Google may use to collect information under its own privacy policy. Our site also links to our Facebook, Instagram and TikTok pages. These services have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for how they handle your information.

Data security and retention

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as long as the law requires us to keep student and training records.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Some records may need to be kept to meet legal requirements. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Children's privacy

Our website and programs are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
