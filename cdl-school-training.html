CDL School FAQs

Answers to the questions students ask us most.

How long does it take to get my CDL?

Many students finish in about 30 days once they are fully enrolled and their paperwork is complete.

When can I start?

Start dates depend on student and instructor availability. Contact us for our next available dates.

Is financial aid available?

You may qualify for funding through the Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act (WIOA) at your local OhioMeansJobs center, especially if you are unemployed or returning to the workforce. Contact us and we can help you get started.

What's the difference between a Class A, B and C CDL?

A Class A CDL lets you drive combination vehicles like tractor-trailers. A Class B CDL covers heavy single vehicles such as straight trucks, dump trucks and buses. A Class C CDL covers smaller vehicles that carry 16 or more passengers or placarded hazardous materials.

What's on the CDL tests?

To get your Commercial Learner's Permit you take knowledge tests, including general knowledge, air brakes and combination vehicles for Class A. The skills test has three parts: a pre-trip inspection, basic vehicle control (backing maneuvers) and an on-road driving test. You must hold your permit for at least 14 days before taking the skills test.

Will I test in the same truck I train in?

Yes. Students generally train and test on the same equipment, so test day feels familiar.

Do I need a drug test?

Yes. A DOT drug test is required before you start training, and it is separate from your DOT physical. Both are included in your program fees.
