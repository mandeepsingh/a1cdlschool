Company Contract vs. CDL School

Whether your employer is paying or you're investing in yourself, you get the same instructors and the same hands-on training.

Company Contract

Who is it for?

Drivers whose employer or future employer is paying for their training.

What do you get?

Class A CDL
  • 25 hrs behind the wheel
  • Online theory
  • Cost: $4,200
Class B CDL
  • 20 hrs behind the wheel
  • Online theory
  • Cost: $3,000

What does everyone get?

  • Hands-on training
  • Experienced instructors

  • Career-focused support
