CDL Testing FAQs

Answers to the questions drivers ask us most.

How do I know if I am ready for my CDL test?

You're ready when you can safely and confidently complete the pre-trip inspection, basic vehicle control and the road test. Our instructors can help you decide if you are ready.

Can I take my CDL test at A1 CDL School?

Yes. We are a third-party CDL testing site for Class A and Class B skills tests.

Do you offer Class A and Class B testing?

Yes, we offer skills testing for both.

Can I use your equipment for my test?

Yes. Truck rental is $250 for Class A or $225 for Class B, added to your test fee.

Can other schools send their students to test here?

In many cases, yes. Contact us to confirm availability and requirements.

Do I have to train with you to test with you?

No. You can test with us even if you trained somewhere else. Contact us to confirm your test.

What does the CDL skills test include?

The full test has three parts: a pre-trip vehicle inspection, basic vehicle control (backing maneuvers) and an on-road driving test.

Can I upgrade from a Class B to a Class A CDL?

Yes. See our Class B to Class A upgrade package, or contact us to schedule your Class A test.

Why test with A1 CDL School?

Affordable pricing, flexible scheduling, experienced staff and test-ready equipment.

How do I schedule my CDL test?

Request an appointment or call (937) 618-5304. We will go over your options, available dates, equipment, cost and what to bring on test day.

Who is eligible to test with A1 CDL School?

Must be a U.S. citizen, OR must be a lawful permanent resident (Green card holder). Permanent residents must possess a valid 10-year Green Card.
